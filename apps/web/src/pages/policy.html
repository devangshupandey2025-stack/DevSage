Founders' Collaboration &
Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

By and Between:

The following individuals (collectively referred to as the "Founders" and individually as a "Founder"):

Whereas
A. The Founders are collaborating to develop a business concept and related technology tentatively known as "[Project / Startup Name]" (the "Project");
B. The Founders intend to incorporate the Project as a private limited company (the "Company") and seek incubation support from [Incubator Name];
C. The Founders wish to establish their respective rights and obligations regarding the Intellectual Property (IP), equity ownership, and management of the Project prior to formal incorporation.
Now, Therefore, the Founders agree as follows:

1.Intellectual Property Assignment

1.1. Assignment of IP

Each Founder hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Project (and effectively to the future Company upon incorporation) all right, title, and interest in and to any and all "Work Product."

1.2. Definition of Work Product

"Work Product" includes, but is not limited to:

  • Source code, algorithms, and software architecture (specifically including [List key tech, e.g., Python backend, LLaMA integration, TypeScript frontend]);
  • Business plans, pitch decks, and financial models;
  • Brand names, logos, and domain names;
  • Customer lists and vendor data.

1.3. No Prior Claims

Each Founder represents that the Work Product contributed by them is original and does not infringe upon the intellectual property rights of any third party, including academic institutions or previous employers.

1.4. Academic Exception

Work submitted strictly for academic credit (e.g., course assignments) remains the property of the student/institution unless it is substantially incorporated into the Project's commercial product, in which case it is assigned to the Project to the fullest extent legally permissible.

2.Equity Ownership & Vesting

2.1. Initial Equity Split

Upon incorporation, the initial shareholding of the Company shall be allocated as set forth in Schedule A (attached hereto).

2.2. Vesting Schedule

The Founders agree that their equity is subject to a "Vesting Schedule" to ensure long-term commitment.

  • Total Vesting Period: 4 Years (48 Months).
  • Cliff Period: 1 Year (12 Months).
  • Cliff Condition: If a Founder leaves the Project for any reason before the 1st anniversary of the Effective Date, they shall forfeit 100% of their equity.
  • Post-Cliff: After 12 months, 25% of the Founder's equity vests immediately. The remaining 75% vests in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months.

2.3. Accelerated Vesting

In the event of a "Liquidity Event" (e.g., acquisition of the Company) prior to full vesting, 100% of the unvested shares shall immediately vest.

3.Roles & Commitment

3.1. Roles

The Founders agree to the following primary responsibilities:

  • [Founder 1 Name] (CEO): Strategy, Fundraising, Investor Relations.
  • [Founder 2 Name] (CTO): Technical Architecture, Code Review, Product Development.
  • [Founder 3 Name]: [Specific Role, e.g., Frontend Development].
  • [Founder 4 Name]: [Specific Role, e.g., Marketing & Growth].

3.2. Time Commitment

The Founders acknowledge they are currently students. However, each Founder agrees to dedicate a minimum of [e.g., 20] hours per week to the Project.

3.3. Moonlighting

Founders may hold internships or other employment provided such activities do not conflict with the interests of the Project or prevent the Founder from fulfilling their time commitment.

4.Decision Making & Dispute Resolution

4.1. Day-to-Day Decisions

Decisions regarding the Project's daily operations shall be made by the majority vote of the Founders.

4.2. Major Decisions

The following actions require a Supermajority Vote (75% of equity ownership):

  • Amending this Agreement;
  • Admitting a new Co-Founder;
  • Diluting equity for investors or incubators;
  • Selling the Project or its assets.

4.3. Deadlock

In the event of a deadlock (a tie vote) that cannot be resolved after 14 days of good-faith negotiation, the decision of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) shall be final and binding.

5.Departure & Termination

5.1. Voluntary Resignation

A Founder may resign by providing 30 days' written notice. Upon resignation:

  • All unvested shares are forfeited to the Company/remaining Founders.
  • Vested shares may be retained by the departing Founder, subject to a "Right of First Refusal" (ROFR) held by the Company to buy back said shares at Fair Market Value.

5.2. Termination for Cause

A Founder may be removed for "Cause" (e.g., fraud, theft of IP, material breach of this Agreement, conviction of a felony) by a majority vote of the other Founders. In the event of Termination for Cause, the Founder forfeits 100% of their equity (both vested and unvested).

6.Confidentiality

6.1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information regarding the Project's business, technology, and finances, including but not limited to: source code, algorithms, customer lists, marketing strategies, and investor term sheets.

6.2. Obligations

Each Founder agrees to:

  • Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
  • Not disclose such information to any third party without the CEO's written consent;
  • Use such information solely for the benefit of the Project.

6.3. Survival

The obligations of this Article shall survive the termination of this Agreement and the departure of any Founder for a period of three (3) years.

7.Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation

7.1. Non-Compete

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year following a Founder's departure, no Founder shall directly or indirectly engage in, manage, or own any business that creates a product substantially similar to or directly competitive with the Project.

7.2. Non-Solicitation of Employees

For a period of two (2) years following a Founder's departure, they shall not induce, solicit, or attempt to hire any other Founder, employee, or contractor of the Project to leave the Project and join a different entity.

8.General Provisions

8.1. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

8.2. Dispute Resolution (Arbitration)

  • Amicable Settlement: In the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Founders shall first attempt to resolve the dispute amicably through good-faith negotiations for a period of fifteen (15) days.
  • Arbitration: If the dispute remains unresolved, it shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
    • Venue: The seat and venue of the arbitration shall be [City, e.g., Chennai], India.
    • Language: The proceedings shall be conducted in English.
    • Arbitrator: The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual consent of the Disputing Parties.

8.3. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the validity, legality, and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not in any way be affected or impaired thereby.

8.4. Entire Agreement

This Agreement (including all Schedules and Exhibits) constitutes the entire agreement between the Founders regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous discussions, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral.

8.5. Amendments & Waivers

  • Amendments: No modification of or amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all Founders (or by Founders holding at least 75% of the equity, if specified in Article 4).
  • Waiver: No failure or delay by any Party in exercising any right, power, or privilege hereunder shall operate as a waiver thereof.

8.6. Notices

All notices, requests, consents, claims, demands, waivers, and other communications hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been given:

  • (a) When delivered by hand (with written confirmation of receipt); or
  • (b) When sent by email to the email address specified by the Founder on the Signature Page, provided that the sender receives no "bounce-back" or error message.

8.7. Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall be deemed to be one and the same agreement.
