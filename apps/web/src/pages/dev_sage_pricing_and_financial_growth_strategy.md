## 2. REVENUE PROJECTIONS (3-YEAR GROWTH)

### Phase 1: Year 1 (The Incubation Year)

**Primary Objective:** User acquisition and MVP validation.  
**Target User Base:** 1,000 active users (Including Participants and Organisers).  
**Revenue Source:** Subscribed Customers  
**Estimated Annual Revenue (ARR):** ₹1,00,000.  
**Growth Driver:** High-touch engagement with VIT student body, Clubs and tech hackathons.

### Phase 2: Year 2 (The Scaling Year)

**Primary Objective:** Monetization and expansion to other technical universities including but not limited to IITs, NITs, and other government and private colleges.  
**Target User Base:** 10,000 active users (5% conversion to Pro).  
**Estimated Annual Revenue (ARR):** ₹5,00,000 - ₹10,00,000.  
**Growth Driver:** Strategic partnerships with GitHub student developer programs and LinkedIn marketing and personal marketing in targeted institutions.

### Phase 3: Year 3 (The Expansion Year)

**Primary Objective:** Market leadership in AI-driven developer tools.  
**Target User Base:** 20,000+ active users.  
**Estimated Annual Revenue (ARR):** ₹10,00,000 - ₹20,00,000.  
**Growth Driver:** Trust based service precendences.

### Phase 4: Year 4 (The Big Consolidation Year)

**Primary Objective:** Continue to Market other institutions while maintaining the website.  
**Target User Base:** 25000+ active users.  
**Estimated Annual Revenue (ARR):** ₹20,00,000 +  
**Growth Driver:** Trust based service precedence and heavy marketing.

## 3. PROJECTED OPERATING EXPENSES (MONTHLY)

Managing the "Burn Rate" is essential for long-term sustainability.

- **Infrastructure (40%)**: Cloudflare Workers/Pages, SQL Database hosting, and LLM API costs.
- **Research & Development (30%)**: Future tool development and refinement of the LLaMA integration.
- **Marketing & Customer Acquisition (20%)**: Campus ambassador programs, social media ads, and event sponsorships.
- **Operational Overheads (10%)**: Legal maintenance of the Founders’ Agreement, tax filings, and office utilities.
